Tips for clean HDRIs
  • Turn the phone around its lens, not around your body: keep your elbows in and pivot your wrists.
  • Put the ring on the black dot and stay still for a moment. Most directions take under a second.
  • The ultra-wide camera needs about a third of the shots. Pick it on the camera screen (on Galaxy phones it's usually the 2nd or 3rd back camera).
  • Outdoors, the sun always clips on a phone. Sun recovery rebuilds its strength so shadows in Blender stay sharp.
  • In Blender: World → Environment Texture → open the .exr. The image centre faces the first direction you shot.
