HDRI Capture

Takes bracketed exposures in every direction and stitches them into a 32-bit 360° HDRI (.exr / .hdr) for lighting 3D scenes.

Checking your phone…

Settings

Main cameras are usually 23–27 mm, ultra-wides 13–16 mm. If it's a bit off you can auto-correct it after capture.

Tips for clean HDRIs

  • Rotate the phone around its lens, not around your body. A tripod or monopod with a pano head works best.
  • Aim the centre ring at the yellow dot. Once you're still, it shoots the whole bracket automatically.
  • Hold still until the dot turns green. If you move, it retries.
  • Avoid moving people and cars. Tall shots with nearby objects show seams most.
  • Pick “Outdoor, sun visible” whenever the sun is in frame so the sun disc isn't clipped.
